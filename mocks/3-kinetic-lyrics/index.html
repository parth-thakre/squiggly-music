Squiggly
Download GitHub

A desktop player for Navidrome.

It plays your own music from your own server.

It asks for the original file,

plays it through libmpv,

and leaves the signal alone.

Records, artists, playlists, favorites.

Search across all of them.

Automatic playlists by genre, by decade, and from what's new.

Radio from any song, record, or artist.

Edit the queue and your playlists.

The queue follows you between devices through the server.

Synced lyrics fill in word by word.

They come from your server.

Looking up missing ones on LRCLIB is off until you turn it on.

Mini player. Tray. Media keys.

The Windows media flyout, MPRIS on Linux.

Files from your computer play with their own cover and tags.

Ctrl+K finds any action.

Keys in keybindings.json, themes as files, extensions as folders of TypeScript that reload when you save.

No EQ.

No crossfade.

No loudness levelling.

No ReplayGain.

Below 100% is attenuation, and it says so.

On Windows it can ask for exclusive output, and reports what mpv accepted.
